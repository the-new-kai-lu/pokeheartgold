# Sample sources for varan revision 3

- Fireplace: **Fire crackling in fireplace**, Davor, https://freesound.org/people/Davor/sounds/382616/ — CC0 1.0. Recorded fireplace crackles.
- Snowstorm: **DMP013016 HEAVY SNOW STORM MS.wav**, martypinso, https://freesound.org/people/martypinso/sounds/35480/ — CC0 1.0. Described by the author as snowstorm, snow gliding on snow, wind and occasional rustling.

Public HQ MP3 previews downloaded, decoded to mono PCM16 at 16,384 Hz, excerpted, filtered and mixed with original synthesized voices. Source URLs and file hashes are in samples/sources.json. Licenses were checked on the authors’ Freesound pages. No existing Pokémon cry is sampled into these mixes.
